About

I am Chetan Suraswal, a video editor who blends technical precision with cinematic storytelling. My workflow goes beyond just making things look good; with a background in data analytics, I approach editing with a deep understanding of pacing, viewer retention, and visual impact. From exacting color space conversions—like perfectly mapping S-Log2 to Rec. 709 in Premiere Pro—to seamless sound design, every frame is crafted with intention. When I am not exporting timelines or finding inspiration on trips into the hills, you can usually find me brewing a strong cup of ginger, tulsi, and honey herbal tea to fuel the next late-night edit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your typical turnaround time?

Turnaround times vary by project scope. Short-form reels typically take 48-72 hours, while comprehensive cinematic edits or commercial cuts may take 1-2 weeks to ensure absolute precision.

Do you handle color grading for raw or log footage?

Yes. I specialize in exact color space conversions, such as mapping S-Log2 to Rec. 709, and applying custom, vibrant color grading to give your raw footage a premium, cinematic look.

How do we handle revisions?

I believe in a highly collaborative process. Standard projects include two rounds of revisions to ensure the pacing, sound design, and overall narrative perfectly align with your vision.

What do you need from me to start a project?

To begin, I will need your high-resolution footage, a brief overview of your goals or the story you want to convey, any specific brand guidelines, and reference videos if you have a particular style in mind.
